Physical media. Real stories.

NEEDLE & REWIND began as a shared crate of records and a shelf of rented-out films in a Camden Passage back room. Today it is a small shop for people who still like to hold what they listen to and watch.

We buy collections big and small, sort them by hand and sell them at fair prices. Some copies are well-known favourites. Others are oddities nobody has asked for in years, and those are our favourites.

This is a fictional shop created for a website demo. The story, names and dates are invented.

How we grade condition

Every copy is checked and graded before it reaches the shelf, and the grade is always shown next to the price.

Near Mint
Looks and plays like new, with barely any signs of handling.
Very Good
Light signs of use. Plays well with minimal surface noise or marks.
Good
Visible wear and some noise or marks, but fully playable and complete.
Acceptable
Heavy wear. Playable, but best for completists and bargain hunters.

What we do

We sell vinyl, CDs, DVDs and Blu-rays, second-hand and always honestly described. Browse the music and film shelves, or come and see us.
